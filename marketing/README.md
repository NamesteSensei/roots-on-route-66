# SweetRoots Marketing Assets

This directory contains official SweetRoots QR code branding assets.

## Folder Structure

### posters/
Large promotional QR posters for:
- events
- storefronts
- social shares
- presentations

### stickers/
Sticker-sized QR graphics.

### business_cards/
Business card compatible QR layouts.

### plant_tags/
Printable labels and grow tags.

### social_media/
Instagram/Facebook optimized graphics.

### source/
Editable originals and generation scripts.

## Main Website

https://roots-on-route-66.vercel.app

## Usage

All QR codes point directly to the live SweetRoots application.

Designed for:
- mobile scanning
- print marketing
- customer onboarding
- digital business cards
- farmer market promotion
